Privacy & Performance

Engineered for macOS. Built for privacy.

Every subsystem is tailored to run natively, silently, and securely on your Mac hardware.

100% On-Device Privacy

Your meeting audio is your organization's most sensitive data. With whisper.cpp compiled for Apple Metal and the Neural Engine, all speech processing runs locally. Zero bytes of audio ever leave your Mac.

ScreenCaptureKit Dual Audio

No kernel extensions or third-party audio drivers like BlackHole. Uses Apple's official ScreenCaptureKit to record system and mic audio with zero latency.

Intelligent Speaker Diarization

Distinguishes 'You' from meeting attendees automatically using on-device acoustic energy analysis and voice embeddings.

Persian & Bilingual Excellence

Powered by Whisper Large-v3 Turbo with dedicated tuning for conversational Persian, engineering terms, and seamless English code-switching.

Apple Calendar Sync

Automatically inspects active events in Apple Calendar via EventKit to pre-fill meeting titles, participant lists, and context.

Action Items & Smart Summaries

Transform hours of discussion into concise minutes tailored for Scrum standups, 1-on-1s, and technical reviews. Export seamlessly to Word, PDF, Notion, or Slack with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mirza Benevis on macOS.

Does transcription really work without an internet connection? +
Yes! The speech recognition engine is based on whisper.cpp and runs entirely locally on your Mac's GPU (Metal) and Neural Engine. It transcribes with full accuracy even in airplane mode with Wi-Fi disabled.
How is system audio captured without virtual drivers like BlackHole? +
Mirza Benevis uses Apple's official ScreenCaptureKit framework in macOS 14+. This framework allows native apps to tap into application audio streams directly at the OS level without third-party audio drivers.
Does it run on Intel Macs as well as Apple Silicon? +
Yes. Mirza Benevis is compiled as a Universal 2 binary, natively supporting both Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) and Intel-based Macs running macOS 14.0 or newer.
Is a Gemini API key required for the application to function? +
Core speech-to-text, speaker diarization, audio mixing, and export work 100% offline without any API key. The optional Gemini API key is only required for generative linguistic features: AI meeting summaries, automatic task extraction, and in-meeting Q&A chat. You can get a free key from Google AI Studio.
Is Mirza Benevis completely free? +
Yes. Mirza Benevis is 100% free and open-source software under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, ads, or paywalled features.
